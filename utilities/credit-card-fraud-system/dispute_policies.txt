POLICY: Credit Card Dispute Resolution Guidelines

Section 1 - Unauthorized Transactions
Transactions made on new devices between 11pm and 5am 
are classified as high-risk unauthorized disputes.
Refund must be processed within 7 working days if 
fraud score exceeds 0.7 and customer has no prior 
dispute history in last 90 days.

Section 2 - Merchant Error Disputes  
If merchant dispute rate exceeds 5%, automatically 
rule in customer's favour without further investigation.
Amounts below INR 5000 are auto-refunded immediately.

Section 3 - Escalation Rules
All disputes above INR 50,000 require human review 
regardless of fraud score.
Customers with more than 3 disputes in 90 days are 
flagged for enhanced verification before any refund.

Section 4 - Resolution Timeframes
Standard disputes resolved within 30 days per RBI guidelines.
High-value disputes above INR 1 lakh resolved within 7 days.
Customer must be notified within 24 hours of dispute receipt.
