COLLECTIONS POLICY AND SCRIPTS

Section 1 - Contact Strategy
Accounts overdue 1-30 days: SMS and email reminder only.
No outbound calls before 10am or after 6pm.
Maximum 3 contact attempts per day per account.

Section 2 - Settlement Authority
Call centre agents can offer maximum 5% waiver on overdue interest.
Settlement above 5% requires relationship manager approval.
Principal waiver not permitted at call centre level.

Section 3 - Promise to Pay
All promise-to-pay commitments must be logged within 30 minutes of call.
If promise broken twice, account escalated to legal team automatically.
Follow-up call mandatory within 24 hours of missed promise date.

Section 4 - Escalation Rules
Accounts overdue more than 90 days referred to legal team.
Customers with 3+ broken promises flagged as high risk.
Home loan accounts require RM notification within 24 hours of contact.

Section 5 - Legal Escalation
Legal referral requires: overdue amount above INR 50,000 AND
overdue days above 90 AND minimum 2 contact attempts made.
